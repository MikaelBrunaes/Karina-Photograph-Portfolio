About

Karina has spent the last decade photographing weddings across the country, drawn always to the quiet, unscripted moments that a formal portrait never quite manages to hold — a father's hand tightening before the walk down the aisle, laughter breaking through happy tears, the first private glance between two people who just promised each other everything.

Her approach is unobtrusive by design. Rather than directing every frame, she moves quietly through the day, trusting that the truest images come from what's already happening. The result is a body of work that feels less like documentation and more like memory itself — honest, warm, and entirely yours.

“Karina didn't just photograph our wedding — she gave us back a day we'll never stop returning to.”

Emma & James, Married 2025
